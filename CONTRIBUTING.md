# Contributing

Thanks for helping improve the catalogue.

## Resource additions

Please open a focused pull request with a small batch of new entries. For every book, include:

- title and author as shown by the linked source
- a direct link that resolves at review time
- a short reason it belongs in the mathematics catalogue
- confirmation that it is not already listed under another title or link

Prefer author-hosted pages, open textbooks, stable library records, or Internet Archive records. A live link alone is not proof that a book is public domain or that a full scan may be redistributed, so describe access conservatively.

## Paid editions

Keep the paid-editions list separate from the free-resource lists and label related-book adverts as commercial. Link book titles directly to their product pages on [russianmathbooks.com](https://russianmathbooks.com/), not retailer or checkout pages. Verify each page against the book's title and author in the live publisher catalogue. Do not advertise offers or downloads without confirming that they are available, or make bestseller claims without independently verifiable evidence.

## Review checklist

- [ ] The change is limited to mathematics resources or catalogue maintenance.
- [ ] New entries are in a small, numbered batch.
- [ ] Titles, authors, and links were checked.
- [ ] Duplicates and broken links were checked.
- [ ] Access and rights language is accurate.
- [ ] Promotional links use the repository's UTM convention where appropriate.
- [ ] No private reports, credentials, or generated bulk output were added.
